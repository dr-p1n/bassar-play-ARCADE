# BASSAR PLAY — Brand sheet v0
Constraints, not commitments. Written down from what the site already does. Review May 2027.

Name and mark: BASSAR PLAY. Footer on every page: BASSAR. Wordmark (pixel BASSAR + mint PLAY
plate) on the landing page only.
Color: black background, off-white text, grey for secondary/italic. Mint #72ffc2 is the only accent,
one mint element per view. No other colors, no gradients.
Type: Archivo (display) and Instrument Sans (everything else), locked. Labels are [SMALL UPPERCASE IN BRACKETS].
Voice: the site is an archive, not a shop. Use: file, record, the list, filed, recovered,
resolved, transmit, the house. Never: sign up, subscribe, newsletter, play now, "!", emoji.
Short declarative sentences, present tense, never explain the horror. Errors stay in voice.
Nothing is for sale. One exception: the Contact page speaks as a human, still terse.
Layout: centered, a lot of black space. Posters show the name only.
Privacy in public: names only (max 18 chars, blank = "anon"). Never contact details.
Don't: gore, stock horror, new colors, fonts or marks.
